# Design brief: harbour village

A harbour village where my grandchildren can explore and fish.

- Track A (iamar style), type town (plus nature), visitors: friends, up to 8 at once.
- Budgets (low tier): 150000 triangles, texture size 1024, texture bytes 67108864, download 100 MB.
- Defaults I chose, please review: A2 (up to 10 at once), D2 (100 MB).
- Features: npc_fisher, quest_first_catch, script_tide_bell.
- Checkpoints: greybox, scale check, paint.
